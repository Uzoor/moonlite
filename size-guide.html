Find your fit.

Our shoes are listed in EU sizes. Measure your foot, match it to the chart, and if you're between sizes we recommend going up.

How to measure

Three steps

01

Trace your foot

Stand on a sheet of paper, heel to the wall, and mark your longest toe.

02

Measure in cm

Measure heel to toe mark. Do both feet in the evening — feet swell through the day.

03

Match & round up

Use the larger foot. Between two sizes? Pick the bigger one for comfort.

Make them last

Care tips

·

Rotate your pairs

Give shoes a day to breathe between wears — it keeps them fresh and holds their shape.

·

Clean gently

Wipe with a soft damp cloth. For suede, use a dry brush — never soak it.

·

Store smart

Keep them out of direct sun and stuff with paper to hold form in the box.

·

Protect early

A water/stain spray from day one saves a lot of cleaning later on.
