# Moon Lite's Footwear — your guide to running the shop

Everything you need is on one page, and every change you make is live for
customers the moment you save it. There's nothing to upload and nothing to send
to anyone.

## Signing in

Go to **moonlitesfootwear.com/admin** and sign in with your email and password.

Save that address as a bookmark on your phone. On an iPhone, tap the share
button and "Add to Home Screen" — it then behaves like an app.

You stay signed in on your own phone or laptop, so day to day you just open it
and you're in. If you're on someone else's device, press **Sign out** when
you're finished.

**Change your password the first time you sign in.** Scroll down to *Your login*,
type a new one, and press **Change password**.

## Adding a pair of shoes

Press **+ Add product** and fill in what you know. Only two things are required:
the name and the price. Everything else you can add now or come back to later.

For the photos, tap the dashed box and choose them from your phone or computer.
You can pick several at once. They're automatically shrunk to a sensible size for
the web, so don't worry about them being too big — a photo straight from your
camera is fine.

The **first photo is the one customers see** in the shop listing. If you want a
different one first, tap it and it jumps to the front.

A few fields worth explaining:

**Subtitle** is for the colourway — "Black / White", "Cream suede". It shows
under the name.

**Old price** puts a strikethrough on the higher price next to the new one, which
is how you show a discount. Leave it empty if there's no sale.

**Badge** is the little label on the corner of the photo — "New", "Bestseller",
"Last pair". Keep it short; two words at most.

**Sizes** — tap every EU size you actually have. Customers can filter by size, so
this matters more than it looks.

**Featured** puts the pair on the front page under "This month's favourites".
Four to eight featured pairs looks best; if you feature everything, nothing
stands out.

Press **Save product** and it's live. If you added photos it takes a few seconds
while they upload — the button will say "Uploading photo…". Don't close the page
until it's done.

## When something sells

You have two options, and the difference matters for your customers.

If you're **getting more soon**, open the pair and tick **Mark as sold out**. It
stays on the site, dimmed, and the button changes to "Ask about restock" — so
customers still message you and you know there's demand.

If it's **gone for good**, delete it with the bin icon.

For everything else, just update the **Stock** number as you sell. Anything at
3 or fewer shows up as "Low stock" in your summary at the top, which is a useful
nudge to reorder.

## Changing your WhatsApp number or store details

Scroll down to **Business details**. This is where your phone number, email,
address, opening hours, social links and delivery wording live.

The WhatsApp number is the important one — **every order on the site goes to
whichever number is in that box**. You can type it however you like
(`08031234567` or `+234 803 123 4567`); it gets tidied up automatically. The
line underneath confirms it, so read it before saving: it should say
"✓ Orders will go to wa.me/234…". If it warns you, the number is incomplete.

Press **Save details** and it's live.

## How orders reach you

There's no cart and no checkout. Every "Order on WhatsApp" button opens WhatsApp
with a message already written — the pair, the size, the colour and the price —
and the customer just presses send.

So orders arrive as WhatsApp messages, and you confirm availability, delivery and
payment in the chat exactly as you do now. Nothing is charged automatically and
no card details ever touch the website.

## Your summary at a glance

The five boxes at the top of the dashboard: **Products** is everything on the
site, **In stock** is what customers can buy right now, **Low stock** is 3 or
fewer left, **Sold out** is what's marked unavailable or at zero, and
**Featured** is how many appear on the front page.

## Things worth knowing

**Everything saves to your own database**, not to the phone you're holding. So
you can add a product on your laptop at home and see it on your phone at the
shop, and nothing is ever lost if your phone is.

**Photos should be roughly square** and shot against a plain background if you
can manage it. It's the single biggest difference between a shop that looks
professional and one that doesn't. Consistent backgrounds across all your pairs
matter more than expensive photography.

**Check your work.** Press **View store ↗** in the top corner to see the site
exactly as a customer does.

**If a save fails**, you'll see a message explaining why. Usually it's the
internet — wait a moment and press save again. If it says your session expired,
just sign in again; nothing is lost.

## If you get stuck

The shop keeps working even if the dashboard has a bad day — customers can always
browse and message you. So there's no emergency: take a screenshot of whatever
you're seeing and send it to whoever set the site up for you.
